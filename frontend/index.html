Government Schemes

Find information about public welfare schemes.

PM-KISAN Scheme

Financial assistance of ₹6,000 per year to eligible landholding farmer families.

Eligibility: Eligible landholding farmer families, subject to exclusions.

Benefits: Three instalments of ₹2,000 per year.

Official Website

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

Provides eligible beneficiaries access to hospital treatment.

Eligibility: Identified eligible families and senior citizens aged 70 and above.

Benefits: Health cover up to ₹5 lakh per family per year under applicable scheme rules.

Official Website

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana

Supports eligible women from poor households in accessing LPG connections.

Eligibility: Adult women from eligible poor households without an existing LPG connection in the household.

Benefits: Deposit-free LPG connection and applicable additional benefits.

Official Website
